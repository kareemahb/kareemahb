## 🧭 About Me
 
I started my career as a **Systems Analyst at Aon**, tracking project risks, cleaning up Azure DevOps records for large-scale migrations, and building Power BI dashboards for stakeholders. Then I moved into **technical customer support**, where I saw the other side of technology: the people relying on it.
 
After years of analyzing systems and reporting problems, I wanted to build the solutions myself. Today I bring **systems thinking** and a **user-first perspective** to full-stack apps that make people's work easier.
 
- 🔭 Building **[DocGap](https://github.com/kareemahb/DocGap)**, my Demo Day project
- 🤖 Learning to build **apps with Swift**
- 💬 Ask me about support workflows, documentation, and turning messy data into clear dashboards

## 🚀 Featured Project
 
### [DocGap](https://github.com/kareemahb/DocGap): find the documentation that's costing your team the most time
 
DocGap links recurring support tickets to the docs behind them, gives each doc a **Documentation Debt Score**, and compares ticket activity before and after a fix so teams can see whether the update actually worked.
 
`React` · `Tailwind` · `Node.js` · `Express` · `MongoDB` · `MVC`
